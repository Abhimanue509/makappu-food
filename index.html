Heritage & Tradition

About Makkappu Foods

Based out of Koottanad P.O., New Bazar, Palakkad, Makkappu Foods is a premier traditional food manufacturing brand in Kerala. Under our flagship brand Alankar Pappadam, we preserve the authentic heritage of Palakkad pappadam making.

Every batch of Alankar Pappadam is processed with strict attention to raw material purity—blending premium Orid flour, rice flour, pure gingelly sesame oil, and mineral-balanced water. Our state-of-the-art hygienic facility ensures consistent quality, crispiness, and signature taste that families across Kerala love.
